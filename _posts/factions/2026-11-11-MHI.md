Morkhelt Heavy Industries (abbreviated as MHI) is a Hydrogen Transportation and Industrial manufacturing company owned by Morkhelt 

MHI’s used to be known as “Hydroco”, a hydrogen fuel distributor, to exploit Ice discoveries in The New Enigma Sector before incorporating into Transport and Industrial Manufacturing 

MHI is a vertically integrated company operating in all areas of the Hydrogen and Heavy Machinery Industry, including exploration, extraction, refining, distribution, manufacturing and trading of Hydrogen and Heavy Equipment

While MHI is currently not associated with either CASA or LUNA, MHI continues to make an effort to trade with both factions and mediate peaceful negotiations and communications between the two factions.

MHI itself continues to research and fund new advancements in technology to help aid in the charting of the New Enigma Sector, often researching newer long-range communication systems via Light Sensors and/or QuantumLink Space-Time Fluctuation. While not the best company for researching spatial telecommunications, they have seen success with the “ARTEMIS” Encrypted Communication System, originally designed for internal communication between MHI locations, now made public by its CEO for use to Civilian and Government entities. 

To date, MHI continues to refine and sell Hydrogen, to civil and government services, while continuing their attempts to make an impact in the Industrial Manufacturing space and Telecommunications space.
